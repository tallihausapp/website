Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

The short version

Tallihaus doesn't collect your information. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, where we can't see it. A few features look things up on public services, mostly government sites; exactly what they send is listed below.

Where your information is stored

Bills, mortgage details, appliances, documents, photos and everything else you add are saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, Tallihaus also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud account so your iPhone and Mac stay in sync. That copy is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy terms; only your Apple account can read it, and we can't. Tallihaus has no servers of its own holding your records. To keep data on one device only, turn off iCloud for Tallihaus in your device's iCloud settings.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export a spreadsheet, Tallihaus creates a file and you choose where it goes, such as iCloud Drive or Files. We never receive a copy.

Camera and photos

Photos of appliance labels, bills, receipts and listings are read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. They're never uploaded to us. Text you paste from a bill email is read on your device too. If a bill's PDF has a password, it's used once to open the file and isn't saved.

Mortgage rates

To show current rates, Tallihaus downloads the public weekly mortgage rate averages from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED). This request contains no personal information, though, like any web request, their servers can see your device's internet address.

Weather tips

If you add your ZIP code, Tallihaus asks Apple's map service for its location once, rounds it to about half a mile, and sends that rounded location to the National Weather Service (weather.gov) to get your forecast. Your address and name are never sent. You can turn weather tips off in Settings.

Recall checks

To check for recalls, Tallihaus sends the brand names of your appliances (for example, "Frigidaire") to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's public recall list. Model and serial numbers aren't sent for recall checks; the matching happens on your device. You can turn recall checks off in Settings.

Find a Pro

When you search for a local pro, Tallihaus asks Apple Maps for that kind of business (for example, "plumber") near your home's location, worked out from your ZIP code. Nothing is sent unless you search, and your name and address are never included. Results come from Apple Maps; Tallihaus doesn't share anything with the businesses listed.

Appliance photos

If you tap See Photos of This Model, Tallihaus opens a DuckDuckGo image search for that appliance's brand, model number and type (for example, "Frigidaire FRFG1723AV Refrigerator"). Nothing is sent unless you tap it, and serial numbers are never included. DuckDuckGo doesn't track searches.

Notifications

Reminders and the weekly summary are created on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Links

Links you open, like a listing website or a contractor's site, open in your browser and follow that website's own privacy policy.

Children

Tallihaus is meant for adults managing a home and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

Delete any record in the app; with iCloud sync on, it's removed from your other devices too. Deleting the app removes what it stored on that device; your iCloud copy can be removed in your device's iCloud settings (Manage Storage). Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens an email in your mail app with the app version, device model and system version filled in. You can read or delete those details before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Contact

Questions? Email tallihaus@outlook.com.
