Dashboard

Your whole home at a glance

Open the app and see what your home costs each month, what's due and what needs attention.

  • Bills, mortgage, maintenance and appliances in one summary
  • Recall alerts and savings found, right at the top
  • A Sunday summary of the week ahead
  • New to the app? A Get Started list walks you through setup
Bills

Every utility bill, without the typing

Snap a photo of a bill or open the PDF. Tallihaus reads the amount, due date and usage for you.

  • What's unpaid and what's due next
  • A reminder before each bill is due
  • Export to a spreadsheet for taxes or budgeting
Bills · Bill Insights

Know what your utilities really cost

Tallihaus works out your true price per kWh, CCF or gallon and tells you why a bill went up: higher prices or more use.

  • A year of prices, month by month
  • Every line on your bill, explained
Mortgage

Your loan, balance and equity

See what you owe, what you've paid off and how much of your home is yours.

  • Your balance goes down on its own after each due date
  • Pay Off Faster: what a little extra each month would save
  • Today's mortgage rates, and when refinancing would actually pay off
  • Escrow, taxes and insurance broken out
Mortgage · Fill in from a document

Set up your loan in seconds

Choose your Closing Disclosure, amortization schedule or latest mortgage statement. Tallihaus reads it and fills in your loan for you.

  • Lender, loan amount, rate, term, payment, escrow and PMI
  • Today's balance, worked out from your loan's schedule
  • Read on your iPhone or Mac. The document isn't uploaded or kept.
Appliances

Know what's aging before it fails

Scan the label on your water heater or fridge and Tallihaus works out its age from the serial number.

  • See photos of the model to make sure it's yours
  • Check today's price for your model, or let Tallihaus estimate it from the model number
  • Recall alerts, checked against the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's list
  • Warranties and model numbers, ready when the repair person asks
Appliances · 5-year forecast

No surprise replacements

See what may need replacing in the next five years, what it will likely cost, and what to set aside each month.

Maintenance

The upkeep, suggested for your home

Tallihaus suggests tasks based on the appliances you have and the season, and reminds you before they're due.

  • Overdue and due-soon tasks, front and center
  • Weather heads-up: simple steps before a hard freeze or heavy rain, from the National Weather Service
  • A log of what you did and when

Celebrate your home

Every year in your home counts

Owning a home is a lot of work, and Tallihaus notices. On the anniversary of the day you got the keys, it shows what you've done since.

  • How much of your mortgage you've paid off, and how your home's value has grown
  • The projects you finished, and what they may add at resale, from the national Cost vs. Value report
  • A card to share the moment, with no dollar amounts
  • A little encouragement after every job, big or small
Your home's story

Look back on everything you've done

Swipe through your home's story on one timeline, from the day you got the keys to today.

  • Every project, with what it cost
  • Anniversaries, highlighted year after year
  • Milestones like owning 25%, then half, of your home, or the day PMI came off
  • How your home's value has grown
Your year at home

See the year, share the wins

Every January, Tallihaus sums up what you spent, saved and fixed, with cards you can share (dollar amounts optional). Selling? Create a Home Passport: a polished report of your home's systems, upgrades and upkeep for buyers and agents.
