Questions

When can I get Tallihaus?

Tallihaus is in testing now. Email tallihaus@outlook.com to join the free beta through Apple's TestFlight app. It will come to the App Store after that.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start.

Where is my information stored?

On your iPhone or Mac. Tallihaus doesn't have servers that hold your records. You can back up to iCloud Drive or anywhere you choose.

Does it connect to my bank or utility account?

No logins needed. Add a bill by taking a photo, opening the PDF, or typing it in, and Tallihaus reads the details for you. Your mortgage works the same way: choose your Closing Disclosure, amortization schedule or a statement and the loan fills itself in.

Is this financial advice?

No. Affordability, refinancing, savings and payoff numbers are estimates to help you plan. Talk with a licensed lender or advisor before you decide.

What devices does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 18 or newer, and Macs running macOS 15 or newer.
