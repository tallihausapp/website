For homeowners

Everything about your home, finally organized

Tallihaus does more than keep records. It watches for the things that cost homeowners money.

Money Finder

Finds money you're leaving on the table

Tallihaus checks your mortgage, property taxes and bills, and tells you what to do about it in plain English.

  • PMI you can drop, or that should have ended already
  • A county property value that looks too high, with the steps to appeal
  • Water bills that jump, a common sign of a leak
  • When refinancing would actually pay off
Bill Insights

Know what your utilities really cost

Snap a photo of a bill or open the PDF. Tallihaus reads it and shows what you really pay.

  • Your true price per kWh, CCF or gallon, and how it's changing
  • Why a bill went up: higher prices or more use
  • Every line on your bill, explained
Appliances

Know what's aging before it fails

Scan the label on your water heater or fridge and Tallihaus works out its age from the serial number.

  • Recall alerts, checked against the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's list
  • A 5-year forecast of what may need replacing, and what to set aside each month
  • Warranties and model numbers, ready when the repair person asks

Questions

When can I get Tallihaus?

Tallihaus is in testing now. Email tallihaus@outlook.com to join the free beta through Apple's TestFlight app. It will come to the App Store after that.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start.

Where is my information stored?

On your iPhone or Mac. Tallihaus doesn't have servers that hold your records. You can back up to iCloud Drive or anywhere you choose.

Does it connect to my bank or utility account?

No logins needed. Add a bill by taking a photo, opening the PDF, or typing it in, and Tallihaus reads the details for you.

Is this financial advice?

No. Affordability, refinancing, savings and payoff numbers are estimates to help you plan. Talk with a licensed lender or advisor before you decide.

What devices does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 18 or newer, and Macs running macOS 15 or newer.
