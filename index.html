Questions

When can I get Tallihaus?

Tallihaus is in testing now. On your iPhone, tap here to join the free beta through Apple's TestFlight app. It will come to the App Store after that.

Does it work where I live?

Yes, anywhere in the United States. Add your ZIP code and Tallihaus uses your state's typical property taxes and the National Weather Service forecast for your area. It never uses your phone's location.

I just downloaded it. Where do I start?

A short Get Started list walks you through it: your ZIP code, your mortgage, your bills, an appliance label and your maintenance tasks. Buying instead? It starts with your budget and the homes you like. Each step takes a minute or two. Want to look around first? Explore a sample home (or sample homes, if you're buying) with one tap, and remove them when you're ready.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start.

Where is my information stored?

On your iPhone, backed up in your own private iCloud account. Tallihaus doesn't have servers that hold your records, and we can't see them.

Does it connect to my bank or utility account?

No logins needed. Add a bill by taking a photo, sharing the PDF from your email (even a password-protected one), or pasting the bill email, and Tallihaus reads the details for you. It reminds you when each new bill should be out. Your mortgage works the same way: choose your Closing Disclosure, amortization schedule or a statement and the loan fills itself in.

Is this financial advice?

No. Affordability, refinancing, savings, payoff and home value numbers are estimates to help you plan. Talk with a licensed lender or advisor before you decide.

Does the tour checklist replace a home inspection?

No. It's a way to take notes while you tour, not an inspection. Always get a professional home inspection before you buy. Find a Pro shows local businesses from Apple Maps; Tallihaus doesn't vet or endorse them. More in the Terms of Use.

What devices does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 18 or newer, and Macs running macOS 15 or newer.

How do I send feedback or report a problem?

In the app, open Settings & Backup (or the menu at the top of the home screen) and tap Send Feedback. It starts an email with your app version and device filled in, so we can look into it quickly.
