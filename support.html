Support

We're glad to help.

The quickest way: in the app, open Settings & Backup and tap Send Feedback. It fills in your app version and device for you. Or email tallihaus@outlook.com and tell us your device (for example, iPhone 15) and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

Where is my information stored?
On your devices, synced through your own private iCloud account. Tallihaus has no account and no servers that hold your records.

How do I move to a new phone?
Sign in to the same Apple account and install Tallihaus. Your records come over through iCloud on their own. You can also make a backup file anytime in Settings & Backup.

My utility doesn't send a PDF. How do I add the bill?
Open the "your bill is ready" email, select all the text, copy it, and tap Paste in a new bill. Or take a screenshot of the bill in your utility's app or website and choose it with Scan Bill → Choose from Photos.

My bill PDF asks for a password.
Share it to Tallihaus and enter the password when asked. It's often your ZIP code or the last digits of your account number; the email usually says. The password is only used to open the file and isn't saved.

Why didn't a label or bill scan fill everything in?
Scans work best with a sharp, well-lit photo taken straight on. You can always fill in or fix any field by hand.

Does it work outside Ohio?
Yes, anywhere in the U.S. Add your ZIP code (Your Home on the dashboard, or the Afford tab when buying) for your state's typical property taxes and local weather alerts.

Are the mortgage numbers advice?
No. Affordability, rates, refinance and payoff figures are estimates for planning. Talk with a licensed lender or advisor before you decide.
