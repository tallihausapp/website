Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. It's made by Tallihaus, a home app for homeowners. The binder has a QR code to our free app, which is how it pays for itself.

Can my clients type into it?
Yes. It opens in free Adobe Acrobat Reader or Preview on a Mac, and every line and box can be typed in. Some built-in phone viewers show the pages but won't let you type, so suggest saving it and opening it in Acrobat Reader. It prints on US Letter paper too.

Can I share it with my team?
Of course. Each agent can make their own copy here with their own photo and details.

What if my details change?
Come back and make a new copy. It only takes a minute.
