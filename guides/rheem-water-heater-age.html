How old is my Rheem water heater?

Rheem makes it easy. The first four digits of the serial number are the month and year it was made. The same goes for Ruud and Richmond, which Rheem builds.

How to read it

Rheem's water heater serial numbers are ten characters: two digits for the month, two for the year, a letter for the plant, then a running number.

So 1209D01234 was made in December 2009, and 0322M05678 in March 2022.

Some serials start with letters like R, RN or RHNG. Skip the letters and read the first four digits after them.

Two things to watch

  • Tankless and hybrid models sometimes lay the label out differently. Look for the month and year as the first digits after any letters, and check the rating plate for a printed date.
  • Rheem and Ruud furnaces and air conditioners use a different code (week and year, a few characters in). This page is only for water heaters. See furnace and AC age.

Is it time to replace it?

Most tank water heaters last about 8 to 12 years. If yours is past 10, check the base for moisture and the hot water for a rusty tint once in a while, and start setting money aside.
