Home maintenance checklist

None of this is hard. The hard part is remembering. Here's the short version that covers most houses.

Every month

  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors
  • Check the furnace filter (1-inch filters usually need changing every 1 to 3 months)
  • Run water in any sink or shower you don't use, so the drain trap doesn't dry out

Spring

  • Have the AC serviced before the first hot week
  • Clean the gutters and make sure downspouts drain away from the house
  • Walk around the outside: cracked caulk, peeling paint, loose siding
  • Test the sump pump if you have one (pour a bucket of water in the pit)

Fall

  • Have the furnace serviced
  • Clean the gutters again after the leaves drop
  • Disconnect hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • Check weatherstripping on doors and windows

Once a year

  • Flush the water heater
  • Clean the dryer vent all the way to the outside
  • Recaulk around tubs and showers where it's cracked
  • Replace smoke detector batteries (detectors themselves after 10 years)

Every house is a little different: a well, a septic tank, a fireplace or a deck all add their own jobs. Want it on paper? The free Tallihaus House Binder has a printable maintenance log.
