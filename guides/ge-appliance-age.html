How old is my GE appliance?

The first two letters of a GE serial number are the month and the year. This works for GE, GE Profile, Café, Monogram and Hotpoint fridges, dishwashers, ranges, washers and dryers.

The serial is on a label inside the door or along the frame on most GE appliances: inside the fridge on a side wall, on the edge of the dishwasher door, behind the range's drawer or inside the washer or dryer door.

GE reuses the same 12 letters for the years, so the code repeats about every 12 years. AD could be January 2002, 2014 or 2026. Pick the one that fits the appliance. The print date on the owner's manual helps too.
