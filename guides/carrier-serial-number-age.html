How old is my Carrier furnace or AC?

On Carrier units from the last few decades, the first four digits of the serial number are the week and then the year. Bryant, Payne and Day & Night are made by the same company and use the same code.

So 4419A12345 was built in week 44 of 2019, around the start of November. Watch the order: the week comes first, then the year. Reading it backwards gives a nonsense date like week 19 of 2044.

Weeks to months: weeks 1–4 are January, 5–8 February, 9–13 March, 14–17 April, 18–22 May, 23–26 June, 27–30 July, 31–35 August, 36–39 September, 40–44 October, 45–48 November, 49–52 December.

Older units

Before the switch to week-and-year, Carrier used a letter for the month and one digit for the year (A1 was January 1971), and Bryant used a letter for the year. If your serial starts with a letter, the unit is likely 40 years old or more and well past its expected life.

How long they last

A gas furnace usually lasts 15 to 25 years and a central air conditioner 7 to 15.
