How old is my Bradford White water heater?

Bradford White uses letters, not numbers. The first letter of the serial number is the year and the second is the month.

Year letters

The letters repeat every 20 years, and I, O, Q, R, U and V are never used.

Which 20 years?

Because the code repeats, an M could be 1995 or 2015. The house usually settles it: if the heater looks newer than 30 years, it's the later one. A heater from the 1980s or 1990s will show it: older-style controls, a faded label and often rust at the fittings. Bradford White's warranty lookup on its website can also help if you're unsure.

Examples: HK… is October 2011. DG… is July 2007. AB… made recently is February 2024.
