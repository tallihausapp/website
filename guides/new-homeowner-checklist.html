Just closed on a house? Do these first.

Congratulations. Before the boxes are all unpacked, a few hours on this list will save you a lot of trouble later.

The first week

  • Change or rekey the exterior locks and garage door codes
  • Find the main water shutoff, the electrical panel and the gas shutoff
  • Test every smoke and carbon monoxide detector
  • Change the furnace filter so you know the size and where it goes

The first month

  • Write down the age of the big things: furnace, AC, water heater, roof. The water heater and furnace have it on the label.
  • Go back through the home inspection report and pick out what needs fixing
  • Put the Closing Disclosure somewhere safe. You'll want it at tax time and if you refinance.
  • Set up a folder for receipts from improvements. They can lower the tax you owe when you sell.
  • Check the property tax bill and look into the homestead or owner-occupied credit your state offers

The first year

  • Get the furnace and AC serviced once each
  • Learn when your PMI can come off (see how to get rid of PMI)
  • Start a simple maintenance routine
