How old is my Lennox furnace or AC?

Skip the first two digits of a Lennox serial number. They're the factory. The next two are the year, and the letter after them is the month.

Month letters

A is January, and the letter I is skipped.

AJan
BFeb
CMar
DApr
EMay
FJun
GJul
HAug
JSep
KOct
LNov
MDec

This has been Lennox's format since 1974. Many newer Lennox plates also print the manufacture date, which is the surest answer.

How long they last

A gas furnace usually lasts 15 to 25 years and a central air conditioner 7 to 15.
