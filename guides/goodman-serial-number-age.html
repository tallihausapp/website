How old is my Goodman or Amana unit?

On most Goodman units, the serial number is ten digits and the first four are the year and then the month. Amana and Janitrol heating and cooling equipment is made by Goodman and usually follows the same pattern.

So 1406123456 was made in June 2014.

Check it makes sense

If the first four digits give a month above 12, you're probably looking at an older unit with a different code. Check the plate for a printed manufacture date, or the install date on a service sticker.

Older Amana equipment used a word code (BLACK HORSE, one letter per year in the 1970s and 80s). If yours has that, it's long past its expected life.

How long they last

A gas furnace usually lasts 15 to 25 years and a central air conditioner 7 to 15.
