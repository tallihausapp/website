How old is my A.O. Smith water heater?

On A.O. Smith water heaters made since about 2008, the first four digits of the serial number are the year and the week it was built. State and American heaters, now part of A.O. Smith, use the same idea.

Made in 2008 or later

The serial number is 11 characters. The first two digits are the year, the next two the week of the year.

Weeks to months: weeks 1–4 are January, 5–8 February, 9–13 March, 14–17 April, 18–22 May, 23–26 June, 27–30 July, 31–35 August, 36–39 September, 40–44 October, 45–48 November, 49–52 December.

American, U.S. Craftmaster and Whirlpool water heaters

American Water Heater (owned by A.O. Smith since 2001) builds American, American ProLine, U.S. Craftmaster and Whirlpool water heaters. Their serials also start with the year, then the week: 1745… is week 45 of 2017, early November.

Older A.O. Smith and State heaters

Before 2008 the date was a letter for the month plus a two-digit year.

  • A.O. Smith: the second character is the month letter and the next two digits are the year. H06 in that spot means August 2006 (A = January, B = February… skipping I).
  • State: the month letter comes first. C05… is March 2005.

A heater with one of these older codes is close to 20 years old, well past the 8 to 12 years most tanks last.
