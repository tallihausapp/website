How old is my Trane or American Standard unit?

Trane usually prints the manufacture date on the rating plate, often in the lower right corner, so look there first. If it's missing, the serial number has it.

Made in 2010 or later

The first two digits are the year and the next two are the week.

Weeks to months: weeks 1–4 are January, 5–8 February, 9–13 March, 14–17 April, 18–22 May, 23–26 June, 27–30 July, 31–35 August, 36–39 September, 40–44 October, 45–48 November, 49–52 December.

Made 2002 to 2009

These serials are usually nine characters and the year is a single digit near the start, for example an 8 for 2008. Because it's only one digit, go by the printed date on the plate when there is one.

American Standard is made by Trane and uses the same codes.

How long they last

A gas furnace usually lasts 15 to 25 years, a central air conditioner 7 to 15 and a heat pump 10 to 15.
