Journey

Know exactly where you are

Your whole purchase on one screen: the days until closing, what's next and how far along you are.

  • From browsing to under contract to closing day
  • The next few steps, with due dates
  • Not ready to type anything? Explore four sample homes first
  • A Get Started list for your budget, your ZIP code and the homes you like
Rates

Check today's mortgage rates first

Rates set how much house you can afford, so start here: this week's average 30-year and 15-year rates from Freddie Mac, the last six months, and where rates could go next.

  • What the Federal Reserve is doing, in plain English
  • What a rate change means for your monthly payment
Afford

What you can comfortably afford

Not just what a lender might approve. Tallihaus shows a standard number and a more cautious one, and every step of the math.

  • Taxes, insurance, PMI and HOA included, with property taxes for your area from your ZIP code
  • Your other monthly debts counted in
  • Plain explanations, no jargon
Homes

Save every home you like, or scan the listing

Take a screenshot of a Zillow, Redfin or Realtor.com listing and Tallihaus fills in the price, beds, baths and more.

  • The true monthly cost of each home, with taxes and insurance
  • A Home Score that weighs how much you liked it, the repairs it needs and your budget
  • Your notes, rating and a photo for each one
Tour

Tour each home with a checklist

A walk-through checklist for showings, from the roof to the water heater, so you notice what matters.

  • Mark each item "Looks good" or "Concern" as you go
  • Concerns become repair estimates for that home
  • Outside, inside, kitchen, systems and the neighborhood
Compare

Compare them side by side

Put up to three homes next to each other: price, monthly cost, size, repairs and your rating, with the best in each row highlighted.

  • Monthly cost all-in, not just the price
  • Repairs needed now and within five years
  • Home Scores to see which one really fits
Checklist

From offer to keys, every step

A closing checklist built around your closing date, with reminders so nothing slips.

  • Due dates count back from closing day
  • Movers, utilities, mail, the Closing Disclosure and more
  • A warning to confirm wire instructions by phone, since wire fraud targets buyers
I got the keys

Move in, and nothing is lost

On closing day, one tap switches Tallihaus to homeowner mode. Everything you entered while buying carries over.

  • Your mortgage, set up from the loan you chose
  • Repairs from your tour become planned projects
  • Starter maintenance tasks for your new home
  • Your purchase price becomes your first home value

Already have the keys?

Track bills, the mortgage and upkeep, and find savings you'd miss.
